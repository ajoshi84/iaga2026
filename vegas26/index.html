The Legend Begins

How it started

Twenty-four golfers signed up for IAGA's first-ever Premier Grand Adventure in Las Vegas, and the organizers tracked everything by hand: who was landing Thursday night, who was at the Wynn vs. the Bellagio vs. the Mirage, shirt sizes, and the all-important veggie vs. non-veggie count. Grassroots doesn't begin to cover it.

Thirty-six holes across Paiute Wolf and the exclusive Cascata. Caddies provided at Cascata. $800 championships on the line, $50 skins, and one legendary warning in the announcement email: "Good luck navigating from the table to the tees in a timely manner. Don't get DQ'd for missing your tee time (it won't be the first time that's happened in Vegas)."

The sun set before the golf was done — champions were crowned with the 18th hole still unconquered. But as the awards ceremony wrapped up, Irfan Mehdi looked around the room and called it: "I do believe this is a start of something big in IAGA."

He was right. Year two took over TPC Las Vegas and Rio Secco. Year three: Snow Mountain and TPC Summerlin. Year four: back to Paiute Wolf and Las Vegas Country Club. Year five: Wolf Creek and Snow Mountain — the year the bus joined the party, and the whole crew howled like wolves every time someone said the word "Wolf." Sunil Mayur claimed Augusta and Ketki Mathuria owned the par 3s.

Now it's the 6th Annual — and we're back where it all started: Paiute, the Wolf course. Thirty-two golfers, our biggest field ever, two legendary courses, one bus. Six years after a spreadsheet and a sunset, we're bigger, louder — and still howling at the word "Wolf." The spreadsheets are gone; these days Megatron, The IAGA AI agent handles the heavy lifting.

Time to howl. 🐺

The Weekend Playbook

🎲 Team Match: teams are drawn at random on both days — one Augusta and one Pebble player per team.

🍸

Thu, Oct 15 — The calm before the storm

  • Kickoff night at the Chandelier Bar, Cosmopolitan (optional) — meet, greet, and question your life choices before a single ball is struck.
🐺

Fri, Oct 16 — Day 1: Paiute (Wolf Course)

  • Bus leaves Aria — 9:00 AM sharp. Ride together, arrive together.
  • Breakfast at Paiute — fuel up, you'll need it.
  • Golf: Paiute Wolf Course — 12:30 PM shotgun start (starting holes 1–5).
  • Bus ride back — the official score-roasting session. Good vibes for everyone… well, not really for the leaders. 😏
  • Dinner and evening: on your own — Vegas is your oyster. 🦪
🌊

Sat, Oct 17 — Day 2: Reflection Bay + Awards Night

  • Bus leaves Aria — 9:00 AM sharp. Ride together, arrive together.
  • Golf: Reflection Bay — morning tee times.
  • Lunch at Reflection Bay after the round — at your own time; $25 provided per person.
  • Bus ride back — reliving that missed putt and that pitching-wedge of a drive, all the way to Aria. 🚌
  • Awards dinner at Lemongrass, Aria — 8:00 PM — prizes, glory, and the traditional roasting of the Worst Round winners. 🎉

House Rules

Format: 36-hole individual net tournament across two days, played in flights. Handicaps: NCGA handicap index applies. Max score per hole: 8 on par 3s, 9 on par 4s, 10 on par 5s. Ties share rank.

Day 1: Paiute Wolf Course (Oct 16) — shotgun start  ·  Day 2: Reflection Bay (Oct 17) — tee times

Winners are announced live at the awards dinner and posted on the winners page the moment they're final. Full prize list lives in The Jackpot below.
