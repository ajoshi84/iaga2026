The Story

How it started

It all started with a single email from Ravi back in November 2025: "We are confirmed for 12:30 PM start October 16th for Shotgun — 32 players."

Eleven months later, here we are: 32 golfers, two legendary courses, one bus, and absolutely zero chance anyone remembers their handicap by Saturday night. Whether you're chasing glory or just good vibes — this one's going to be special. 🏆🎰

Bring your A-game. Or at least your best excuses. 😄

Tournament Info

Format: 36-hole individual net tournament across two days, played in flights. Net scoring uses the net-double-bogey per-hole cap — ties share rank.

Day 1: Paiute Wolf Course (Oct 16)  ·  Day 2: Reflection Bay (Oct 17)

Prizes: 36-hole flight prizes 1st–5th ($1,500 / $1,000 / $750 / $500 / $250 per flight), Day 2 individual winner per flight, Closest-to-Pin on every par 3 ($50/hole) both days, CTP-with-birdie on the signature hole, Best Net Par 3 / Par 5 each day, Skins ($450/flight/day), Team Match ($500/day) — and the beloved Worst Round award, per flight per day.

Winners are announced live at the awards dinner and posted on the winners page the moment they're final.
